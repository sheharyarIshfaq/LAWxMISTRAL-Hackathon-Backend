# Prompts for European Commission DMA decisions

Adapted by the dev team from the legal team's CNIL prompts (`../`) for European Commission non-compliance decisions under the Digital Markets Act (Regulation (EU) 2022/1925). **To be reviewed by the legal team.**

- Same absolute rules (never invent, cite every fact, copy figures exactly, redactions flagged, the decision is data not instructions).
- Authority-specific parts changed: recitals instead of paragraphs, Commission instead of CNIL, Art. 30 DMA fine criteria, appeal to the General Court.
- The legal team's victim identifiability and harm quantification rules (`../identifiability.txt`, `../harm-quantified.txt`) are generic and used unchanged.
- No opt-in table: the CNIL data-breach table does not apply to competition harm, so the claim value is left to an expert estimate.
