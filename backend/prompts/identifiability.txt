VICTIM IDENTIFIABILITY

Decide whether the victims are identifiable by answering two questions from the decision:
(a) Does someone hold a list of the victims? (the defendant has a contractual relationship with them, notified them individually, or holds orders, invoices or contracts)
(b) Can each victim prove membership of the group? (notification email or letter, contract, invoice, bank statement, order confirmation)

Levels:
- Yes: both (a) and (b) are met for the whole group.
- Partly: a list exists but the victims were not individually informed, or only part of the group meets both conditions.
- No: victims are diffuse, with no direct relationship to the defendant and no record (e.g. in-store purchases, end consumers buying through intermediaries).

Rules:
1. Base the answer only on the decision. Never assume a list or a proof exists if the decision does not mention it.
2. Name the evidence and cite its source. Example: "Yes: subscribers notified by email between 24 and 29 October 2024 (§ 3)."
3. If only a sub-group is identifiable, give the level "Partly" and name both parts. Example: "Partly: 524,867 patients known to the hospital; 202,246 designated relatives never informed (§ 137)."
4. Flag any wording that weakens identification: "third parties", "former customers", "terminated contracts", or a notification found insufficient by the authority.
