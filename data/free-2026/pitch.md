## 1. The case in three sentences

In October 2024, an attacker used an employee's remote-access credentials to break into the information systems of Free Mobile and Free and extract subscriber data, including IBANs. The CNIL found the companies in breach of their security and victim-notification obligations and fined them €42 million in total. The breach concerns more than 24 million subscriber contracts.

## 2. What the CNIL established

- **Security of processing (GDPR art. 32):** "la procédure d'authentification pour accéder au réseau privé virtuel des sociétés n'était pas suffisamment robuste" (p. 4)
- **Communication to data subjects (GDPR art. 34):** "le courriel adressé aux personnes concernées ne contenait pas l'ensemble des informations requises par l'article 34 du RGPD" (p. 5)

## 3. The group

Subscribers of Free Mobile or Free whose data was included in the October 2024 breach. The CNIL states the size in contracts, not persons: "La violation concerne les données de plus de 24 millions de contrats d'abonnés, dont certains contenaient l'IBAN de l'abonné." (p. 3). One person may hold several contracts.

## 4. The harm

Leaked data: identity, contact details and, for some subscribers, IBAN. This exposes victims to phishing, identity fraud and fraudulent direct debits. The CNIL received complaints reporting phishing attempts (p. 3). **Individual harm must still be proven in court for each member of the group.**

## 6. Risks

- **Appeal:** the decision can be appealed to the Conseil d'État; appeal status is not stated in the decision.
- **Opt-in uncertainty:** actual participation in French group actions is historically low.
- **Low individual harm:** contact-data-only victims may receive little compensation.
- **Group size:** the CNIL counts contracts, not persons.
- **Missing information:** defendant revenue; whether the data was published or sold.

## 7. Compliance statement

The association keeps full control of the action. The funder has no influence over the decision to bring the action or over how it is conducted. The funder's identity and the key terms of the funding agreement will be disclosed publicly.
