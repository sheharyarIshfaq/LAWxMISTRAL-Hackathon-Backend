## 1. The case in three sentences
FREE MOBILE, a private telecommunications provider, suffered a data breach between 28 September and 21 October 2024 via a compromised VPN and internal tool. The attacker accessed data linked to 24,633,469 contracts. The CNIL sanctioned FREE MOBILE with a €27,000,000 fine.

## 2. What the CNIL established
- Storage limitation: "la société conservait donc des données à caractère personnel de millions d’anciens abonnés pendant une durée excessive" (p. 4).
- Security of processing: "la société n’avait pas déployé les moyens suffisants pour être en mesure de détecter les activités suspectes sur son VPN" (p. 15).
- Communication of breach: "le courriel d’information initial n’a pas constitué une communication appropriée" (p. 21).

## 3. The group
All individuals whose data was exposed under the 24,633,469 affected contracts would qualify to join. The CNIL states: "l’attaquant a pu prendre connaissance, des données concernant 24 633 469 contrats" (p. 3).

## 4. The harm
The leaked data includes identity, contact, IBAN, and other information, exposing victims to risks such as fraud or phishing. The CNIL recorded 2,614 complaints: "la CNIL avait reçu 2 614 plaintes de personnes concernées par cette violation de données" (p. 2). Individual harm must still be proven in court.

## 5. Estimated recovery
Recovery is estimated as: people affected × opt-in rate × compensation per person (all inputs are assumptions).

- *Assumption:* opt-in rates for "Violation de données par défaut de sécurité, victimes identifiées" from the legal team's table of past cases (expected 6.8% ± 2.95 pts); €150 per victim.
- *Assumption:* funder share of 30% of gross recovery.
- *Assumption:* the CNIL counts contracts, not persons; each one is treated as one person here.

## 6. Risks
Appeal status is not stated in the decision. Opt-in rates are uncertain. Individual harm may be low or unproven. The defendant is a private telecommunications company. It is unclear whether the data was published or sold.

## 7. Compliance statement
The association retains full control of the action; the funder has no influence over its initiation or conduct. The funder’s identity and key contract terms will be disclosed publicly.
