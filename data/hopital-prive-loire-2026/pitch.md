## 1. The case in three sentences
Hôpital Privé de la Loire, a private healthcare provider, suffered a data breach on 26 June 2025 when an attacker accessed its electronic patient file system using compromised credentials. The breach exposed the personal data of 524,867 individuals. The CNIL imposed a €500,000 fine for regulatory violations.

## 2. What the CNIL established
- Insufficient security measures for remote access, access control, authentication, logging, and third-party access: "l’hôpital n’a pas mis en œuvre un niveau de sécurité adapté." (p. 10)
- Failure to notify 202,246 designated trusted third parties: "aucune information n’a été délivrée aux 202 246 personnes désignées comme tiers de confiance par des patients de l’hôpital" (p. 18)

## 3. The group
The group would include the 524,867 affected individuals (patients and their designated trusted third parties) identified by the CNIL.

## 4. The harm
The leaked data includes identity, contact, and health information, exposing victims to risks such as identity theft, fraud, or misuse of sensitive health data. Individual harm must still be proven in court.

## 6. Risks
- Appeal status is not stated in the decision.
- Opt-in rates are uncertain (assumption).
- Individual harm must be proven in court; only 9 complaints were reported: "la CNIL a reçu neuf plaintes de personnes concernées par cette violation" (p. 3).
- The defendant is a private healthcare provider; its financial capacity is unknown (annual revenue not stated).
- It is unclear whether the data was published or sold (missing information).

## 7. Compliance statement
The association retains full control of the class action. The funder has no influence over its initiation or conduct. The funder’s identity and key contract terms will be disclosed publicly.
