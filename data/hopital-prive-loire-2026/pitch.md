## 1. The case in three sentences
Hôpital Privé de la Loire, a private healthcare provider, suffered a data breach on 26 June 2025 when an attacker accessed its electronic patient file system using compromised credentials. The breach exposed the personal and health data of 524,867 individuals. The CNIL imposed a €500,000 fine for regulatory violations.

## 2. What the CNIL established
- Insufficient security measures for remote access, inadequate access control, and lack of multi-factor authentication: "l’hôpital n’a pas mis en œuvre un niveau de sécurité adapté." (p. 10)
- Failure to notify 202,246 designated trusted third parties: "aucune information n’a été délivrée aux 202 246 personnes désignées comme tiers de confiance par des patients de l’hôpital" (p. 18)

## 3. The group
The group would include the 524,867 affected individuals whose data was compromised, as stated by the CNIL. Trusted third parties (202,246) may also qualify if their data was exposed.

## 4. The harm
The leaked data includes identity, contact, and health information, exposing victims to risks such as identity theft, fraud, or discrimination. Individual harm must still be proven in court.

## 6. Risks
- Appeal status is not stated in the decision.

- Fraud, phishing, or data publication/sale status is not stated in the decision.
